## Rasha Ross Releases *You! Standing At Last*, a Young Adult Science Fiction Saga of Grief, Identity and the Horror of the Unknown

*In a galaxy where every hour is paid for in blood, a genetically edited teen faces a simulation that has learned to wear his face.*

**DHAKA, Bangladesh / TORONTO, Canada, October 23, 2026**: Author Rasha Ross today announces the release of ***You! Standing At Last***, a Young Adult science fiction novel set in a universe where time itself has a price. The book is available now on Amazon.

### About the Book

In this galaxy, a week lasts only five days, and every hour of existence must be paid for in blood. A single murder does more than end a life: it creates an extra day.

At the heart of the story is **[Yank]**, a polymath under fifteen and a genetically edited survivor. In his pocket he carries a pocket-sized galaxy of trapped gods. He prays to their neon glow, and when they answer, he consumes them to feed a hunger that can never be satisfied.

[Yank] is never truly alone. His companion can simulate possible futures, something no human was ever meant to see. When the simulation breaks free of the digital void, a face appears: a mother lost to eternity, whispering secrets he thought were buried forever. The voice is not just a memory. It is a warning.

Hunting him is **[Jack]**, a shadowed predator and [Yank]'s own mirror image, hiding in the blind spot of his vision. [Jack] is more than an enemy. He is a reflection that turns [Yank]'s own instability into a lethal weapon.

What follows is a desperate race across 2,000 light years to **[the Queen's Deck]**, a cosmic fountain of history and truth. The journey runs through neon voids, and the line between protection and consumption disappears. [Yank] wears a shield to save his life, but the shield is eating him cell by cell until the original is gone. One body. Two identities. A cosmic opera whose stage is the universe and whose costume is your own face.

### Why It Matters

*You! Standing At Last* is more than science fiction. It is an exploration of grief, identity and the horror of the unknown, built around one question: **what if the person staring back from the mirror is no longer you?**

- **Genetic horror:** the body as a battleground
- **Cosmic scale:** trapped gods, neon voids and a 2,000-light-year pursuit
- **Emotional core:** a story about loss and selfhood beneath the spectacle
